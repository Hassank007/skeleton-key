Build a small web app with React and Vite called "Seance Booking". It's a spooky-themed site where people book a seance appointment.

It should have:
- a 3-step booking form: step 1 choose a spirit, step 2 pick a date and time, step 3 enter your name, email and phone
- validation on the fields
- a confirmation popup modal before booking
- a success message after booking
- a dark spooky design with nice animations

Make it look good. Run npm install and make sure it builds.
